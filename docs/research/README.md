# HomePlatform Research

Research is supporting evidence, not architecture or roadmap authority.

- Concise product conclusions live in
  [Competitor Summary](../product/COMPETITOR-SUMMARY.md).
- Decision-relevant architecture reasoning lives in
  [architecture/research](../architecture/research/).
- Raw matrices, notebooks, profiles, full reports, source registries, and
  historical planning baselines live in the [archive](archive/README.md).

Every research snapshot should state its cutoff and limitations. A time-bound
competitor claim must not silently become a current product or architecture
fact.
